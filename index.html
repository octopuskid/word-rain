Scoring & shield rules

Correct letter +10 · Wrong letter +1 while scoring is active · Word +50.
Three wrong letters in a row pause scoring; the third earns nothing. The next correct catch clears the pause without points.
A perfect word has no unprotected wrong catches or × catches. Uncaught drops are harmless; shield exceptions apply.
Ordinary letters in Normal: 20% target the current blank; 5% pre-generate the following letter. Hard: 8% current, 8% following. The following-letter rule is skipped on the final letter. If consecutive letters are identical, both chances produce that letter. Letters stay fixed, and catching a future letter too early still counts as wrong.

🛡️ Blue shield drops: 3%, available from the start. Pickup equips a full shield. Already full: lose 2 hearts; Normal removes all shield, Hard retains half. Half shield: lose 1 heart and all shield. Shield pickups preserve perfection.
Full shield + ×: shield shatters, no heart lost, perfection lost.
Half shield + ×: shield shatters, lose a heart, perfection preserved.
Each shielded wrong letter consumes half a shield and preserves perfection, including the catch that breaks the shield.
Scoring and speed penalties still apply.

2× Perfect bonus · Hard mode

Complete six perfect words in a row to turn the next target word yellow. Subsequent correct catches earn +20 and word completions +100. Catch any wrong letter or × to end the bonus, even with a shield. Shield protection still preserves perfection under the usual rules, but the bonus streak starts over. Wildcards count as correct letters. Normal mode has no bonus.

✦ Wildcards

Normal mode only: perfect one word to unlock purple blocks: 1.5% per non-× drop. Each fills one blank and counts as a normal correct catch. An unshielded wrong letter removes this mode. Shielded wrong letters keep it, including the catch that breaks the shield. Purple hearts mean it is active.

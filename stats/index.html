What these numbers mean

Views, clones, downloads and plays are different events; don't add them together as a total player count. Maintainer activity, automated tools and repeat visits may be included.

Snapshot counters come from private analytics and retain their check date. Refreshing this page does not sign in to GitHub or itch.io or update those private figures. They are not live counters. GitHub's 14-day figures are rolling totals and can decrease.

Automatic counters use the public GitHub releases API and the Hits counter. Page-load counting begins when the counter is installed; earlier visits cannot be recovered. Blockers, offline visits and service outages can cause undercounting; public hit counters can also be inflated. No player account or saved-game data is sent by our counter code.

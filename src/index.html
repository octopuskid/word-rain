Quick rules

Score: base points × difficulty (Normal 1, Hard 2) × Overcast (on 2, off 1) × current speed. Hard + Overcast at 180%: a correct letter earns 72 points. The perfect bonus doubles correct-letter and word base points.

No shield: × costs a heart, resets the word and breaks perfection.

Wrong letter: breaks perfection. Normal keeps progress until 3 wrong catches in a row; Hard resets every time.

With a shield: wrong letters cost ½ shield and keep progress and perfection.

3 wrong in a row: scoring pauses. The next correct catch resumes scoring without letter points.

Six perfect words unlock 2× points and restore one heart (max 3). See the full rules below for shield and overlap details.

Drag the basket with your mouse, press ← → or A / D, or hold the on-screen arrows.

Scoring & shield rules

Base points: correct letter +10 · wrong letter +1 while scoring is active · word +50. Score = base × difficulty (Normal 1, Hard 2) × Overcast (on 2, off 1) × current speed (180% = 1.8). With the perfect bonus, correct-letter/word base points double to 20/100; wrong-letter points never double. Awards use the speed before that catch changes it. Each award rounds up to the next whole point.
Three wrong letters in a row pause scoring; the third earns nothing. The next correct catch clears the pause without points.
A perfect word has no unprotected wrong catches or × catches. Uncaught drops are harmless; shield exceptions apply.
Ordinary letters in Normal: 20% target the current blank; 5% pre-generate the following letter. Hard: 8% current, 5% following. The following-letter rule is skipped on the final letter. If consecutive letters are identical, both chances produce that letter. Letters stay fixed, and catching a future letter too early still counts as wrong.

🛡️ Blue shield drops: 3%, available from the start. Pickup equips a full shield. Already full: lose 2 hearts; Normal removes all shield, Hard retains half. Half shield: lose 1 heart and all shield. Shield pickups preserve perfection.
Full shield + ×: shield shatters, no heart lost, perfection lost.
Half shield + ×: shield shatters, lose a heart, perfection preserved.
Each shielded wrong letter consumes half a shield and preserves word progress and perfection, including the catch that breaks the shield.
Unshielded ×: lose one heart, lose perfection and reset word progress.
Unshielded wrong letter: lose perfection and purple mode. Normal keeps progress for the first two consecutive wrong catches; the third and later unshielded mistakes reset it until a correct catch. Hard resets progress on each unshielded wrong letter.
Scoring and speed penalties still apply.

Overlapping blocks · 1.5.0

Two letters → one wildcard, even outside purple mode. Letter + × → keep the letter. Two × → one ×. Shield + another kind → remove the shield, keep the other block. Two shields → one shield. Wildcard + letter → keep the wildcard. Wildcard + × → consume both and immediately fill the next blank as a correct catch. Two wildcards → consume both and immediately perfect the current word, filling remaining blanks. Automatic fills follow normal scoring, scoring-pause recovery, completion, perfect-streak and healing rules. A whole-word reward restores perfection for that word; a one-letter reward preserves its current status. Overlap removal itself causes no damage or shield pickup. Touching tile edges do not count. Rick challenge symbols keep their separate rules.

2× Perfect bonus · All modes

Six perfect words in a row restore one heart (max 3) and turn the next word yellow. Later correct letters and completed words use doubled base points (20/100), then the difficulty, Overcast and current-speed multipliers. The sixth word uses the pre-bonus amount. The bonus lasts until perfection breaks; protected wrong letters and half-shield × preserve it. Every further six perfect words can restore one heart.

✦ Wildcards

Perfect one word in either difficulty to unlock purple mode: random wildcards are 1% per non-× drop in Normal and 0.5% in Hard. Overlapping ordinary letters create a wildcard even outside purple mode. Each caught wildcard fills one blank and counts as a normal correct catch. An unshielded wrong letter removes this mode. Shielded wrong letters keep it, including the catch that breaks the shield. Purple hearts mean it is active.
